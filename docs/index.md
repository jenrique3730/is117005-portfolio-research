## Research question

**Which technology and AI career options are growing, what do people in those roles actually do, and how can someone prepare to enter them?**

This portfolio will track roles such as AI and machine-learning engineering, data science, AI product management, model evaluation, AI governance, cybersecurity, and data infrastructure. The list is a starting point for investigation, not a claim that every title is new or equally in demand.

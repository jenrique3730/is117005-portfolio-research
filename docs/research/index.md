# Career landscape

## What this project investigates

AI is changing work across established occupations while also creating specialized teams and titles. This research compares **job titles** with the underlying **tasks and skills** so that a changing label does not get mistaken for a brand-new profession.

For each career, the project will record:

- Common title variants and where the role sits in an organization
- Core responsibilities, tools, and collaboration partners
- Skills employers request and realistic ways to demonstrate them
- Education, experience, and alternative entry routes
- Demand signals, geography, and the date and limits of each source
- Related roles and possible next steps

## Initial role families

| Family | Roles to investigate | Questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Build AI systems | Machine-learning engineer, AI engineer, research engineer | What software and modeling skills are expected? |
| Work with data | Data scientist, analytics engineer, data engineer | How are data preparation and deployment split across teams? |
| Evaluate and operate models | Model evaluator, AI quality engineer, LLM operations engineer | How do teams test reliability, safety, and production performance? |
| Apply AI to products | AI product manager, AI solutions architect | What technical depth and customer skills are useful? |
| Govern and secure AI | AI governance analyst, AI risk specialist, AI security engineer | Which policy, security, and audit needs are becoming regular work? |

These are research categories, not a ranking of fastest-growing jobs. Growth claims will be added only with a named source, a measured period, and a clear distinction between occupational projections and changes in job-posting titles.

## Research plan

1. Select a small set of roles and collect recent job descriptions from multiple employers.
2. Compare repeated responsibilities, qualifications, and title variants.
3. Check demand against official occupational projections and clearly dated industry reports.
4. Publish a profile for each role with evidence, caveats, and practical career pathways.
